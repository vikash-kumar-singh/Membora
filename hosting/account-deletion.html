Delete your account and data

You can request permanent deletion from inside Membora at More → Privacy & account → Delete account and data, or use the email request below.

Email a deletion request

What to include

  • Send the request from the email address registered to your Membora account.
  • Include your organization name and whether you are an owner or invited staff member.
  • Do not include your password, full identity-document numbers, or payment-card details.

What happens next

We may ask for limited information to verify account ownership. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and will confirm when the request is complete.

  • Organization owner: we will delete the account, device registrations, owned workspace records, uploaded files, and associated operational data, unless ownership is transferred or specific records must be retained by law.
  • Invited staff: we will delete the staff user account, device registrations, and workspace access. Business records created on behalf of the organization remain under that organization’s control where they are not the staff member’s personal account data.

Backups and service-provider systems may take additional time to expire under their retention schedules. Data retained for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or another legal obligation will be isolated from normal use and deleted when the obligation ends.

Deleting a member record: if you are a member of an organization that uses Membora but do not have a Membora login, contact that organization first. You may also email privacy@membora.app and identify the organization so we can assist.
