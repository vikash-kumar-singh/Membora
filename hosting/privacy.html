Membora

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Membora ("Membora", "we", "us") handles information when organizations and their authorized staff use the Membora Android application and related services. Membora is a membership-management service for organizations. The organization using Membora controls the member information it enters, and Membora processes that information to provide the service.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, authentication provider, user ID, role, and staff invitation details.
  • Organization information: organization name, logo, contact details, address, tax identifier, branches, plans, services, and staff configuration.
  • Member and operational records: names, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, dates of birth, gender, emergency contacts, notes, membership history, attendance, commitments, communications, and custom fields entered by an organization.
  • Payments: amounts, payment method, transaction reference, status, date, notes, and receipts. Membora does not collect or store full payment-card numbers.
  • Photos and documents: profile photos, organization logos, identity documents, medical certificates, membership agreements, and other files deliberately uploaded by an authorized user.
  • App and device data: app interactions, notification registration identifier, app version, device and operating-system information, diagnostics, crash traces, and installation identifiers.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; operate and synchronize organization workspaces; manage memberships, attendance, payments, receipts, staff access, files, and reminders; deliver notifications and organization-requested WhatsApp or SMS messages; prevent abuse and duplicate records; troubleshoot crashes; understand feature use; protect the service; and comply with applicable law.

When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers that process data on our behalf, including Google Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Analytics, Crashlytics, and Cloud Messaging), Google identity and barcode-scanning services, hosting and infrastructure providers, and—when configured by an organization—Meta WhatsApp and MSG91 for message delivery. Legacy media may be served through ImageKit. Information may also be disclosed when legally required, to protect rights and security, or as part of a business transfer with appropriate safeguards.

Permissions and on-device access

Camera or photo-picker access is used only when a user chooses to capture or select an image or document. Selected images are cropped on the device before upload. Bluetooth access is used only to connect to a user-selected receipt printer. Notification permission is used for workspace and automation updates. Membora does not request device location, contacts, microphone, SMS-reading, or broad photo-library permissions.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Firebase services encrypt supported data at rest. Workspace access is restricted through authentication and role-based security rules. Private documents are stored as authenticated storage references rather than public download links. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we maintain reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information handled.

Retention and deletion

Workspace data is retained while the organization uses Membora. When a verified account-deletion request is completed, we delete or de-identify the account and associated personal data unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, tax, accounting, or other legal obligations. Records retained for a legal obligation are isolated from normal product use and deleted when that obligation ends. Service-provider backups may take additional time to expire under provider retention schedules.

Organization member data is controlled by the organization that entered it. Members seeking access, correction, or deletion should normally contact that organization first. They may also contact us and we will route or assist with the request where appropriate.

Delete a Membora account: use More → Privacy & account → Delete account and data in the app, or follow the public account-deletion instructions.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to processing of, or delete personal information. You may deny optional permissions in Android settings. Authorized workspace users can update many records directly in the app.

Children

Membora is business software intended for organization owners and authorized staff aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children and should not be used by children to create accounts.

International processing and changes

Service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country, subject to their contractual and legal safeguards. We may update this policy as the product or law changes. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date and, where appropriate, an in-app or service notice.

Contact

For privacy questions or rights requests, email privacy@membora.app. Do not send passwords, full identity-document numbers, or payment-card details by email.
