# Closed-test plan

Use this record if the Play Console account is subject to the 12-testers-for-14-days requirement.

## Setup

- Track: Closed testing
- Testers required: at least 12 continuously opted in
- Minimum duration: 14 consecutive days
- Feedback channel: Play private feedback plus a monitored support channel
- Test data: fictional organizations and fictional members only

## Tester coverage

Ask every tester to complete sign-in, organization creation, navigation and basic member management. Split the remaining scenarios across devices so the group covers:

- profile camera/gallery selection, crop and zoom
- member document upload and viewing
- duplicate-member prevention
- plan, membership and payment creation
- receipt generation, share and printing
- QR and manual check-in
- reports and organization branding
- staff invitation and role restrictions
- reminder automation and messaging-provider states
- offline/reconnect behavior
- privacy policy and account-deletion request path
